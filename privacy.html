Our Commitment to Privacy

ReMa Apps respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications may collect, use, store, and share information when you use our apps.

Information Our Apps May Use

Depending on the features of a particular ReMa application, the app may request access to information or device capabilities needed to provide its functions. This may include files or media selected by you, camera access, notifications, network information, or information you enter directly into the app.

Access to device features is requested only when needed for the relevant app functionality and is subject to Android permissions and your choices.

Information Stored on Your Device

Some ReMa Apps store settings, preferences, history, records, or other app data locally on your device. The exact information stored depends on the application and the features you choose to use.

Advertising

Some ReMa Apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, and interactions with advertisements in accordance with their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

In-App Purchases

Some ReMa Apps may offer purchases through Google Play. Payment transactions are processed by Google Play. ReMa Apps does not directly receive or store your complete payment-card information.

Third-Party Services

Our applications may use third-party services necessary to provide app functionality, advertising, purchases, analytics, or platform services. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Permissions

A ReMa app may request Android permissions such as camera, notifications, storage or media access, or network-related permissions when those permissions are required for a feature. You can review and manage app permissions through your Android device settings.

Data Sharing

ReMa Apps does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate app features, serve advertisements, process purchases, or comply with legal obligations.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to design our applications with privacy and security in mind. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Unless a particular ReMa application and its store listing specifically state otherwise, our apps are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our applications, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or a ReMa application, please contact ReMa Apps using the support contact provided on the applicable Google Play Store listing.
